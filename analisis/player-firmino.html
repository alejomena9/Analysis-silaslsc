Nick Firmino

#10 → False Right Winger
In One Line

Moved off a heavily-marked #10 spot to a false right-winger role, Firmino stopped being the one forced to create — and started being the one defenses can't track.

A Difficult Start As A #10

Nick Firmino began the season as a #10 in a 4-2-3-1, playing just behind the striker. In that role he wasn't finding success — he was being tightly marked by rival midfielders and defenders, struggled to receive behind the opposing double pivot, and simply wasn't influential in the way the team needed. He lost a lot of balls, was constantly in positional inferiority, and misplaced far too many passes.

The Move To False Right Winger

Later in the season the coaching staff moved him to a false right-winger role. When the team has the ball, he tucks inside as an interior, and together with the two 6s and Braudilio — who drops into that #10 space — he forms a diamond shape centrally. That positioning lets him receive with far more freedom, since he's no longer camped in a marked zone but arriving into space as it opens, which generates room not just for himself but for Braudilio as well.

Receiving In Superiority, Not Inferiority

The role change also lets him receive far more often in positional superiority, which in turn gives him the platform for shots from range and considerably more presence inside the box. He's no longer the one who has to single-handedly generate the team's play — that responsibility is now shared across the diamond that forms around him.

A Flank Defenders Can't Solve

This shift also opens real space out wide, because the opposing full-back no longer knows whether to track Firmino as he tucks inside or hold his width and wait for the overlapping full-back to arrive.

Why It Matters

A single positional tweak turned a heavily-marked, low-impact #10 into a free, high-superiority interior — and the ripple effects show up all over the pitch.

Un inicio difícil como #10 A Difficult Start As A #10

Perdía muchos balones, se encontraba constantemente en inferioridad posicional y erraba demasiados pases. Nick Firmino began the season as a #10 in a 4-2-3-1, playing just behind the striker. 02

El movimiento al falso extremo derecho The Move To False Right Winger

Más adelante en la temporada, el cuerpo técnico lo trasladó a un rol de falso extremo derecho. Cuando el equipo tiene el balón, Firmino se mete hacia dentro como interior y, junto con los dos 6 y Braudilio —que baja hacia el espacio del #10— forma un rombo central. Esta posición le permite recibir con mucha más libertad, porque ya no permanece en una zona constantemente marcada, sino que llega al espacio cuando este se abre. Esto genera espacio tanto para él como para Braudilio. Later in the season the coaching staff moved him to a false right-winger role. Recibir en superioridad, no en inferioridad Receiving In Superiority, Not Inferiority

Eso le proporciona la plataforma para buscar disparos desde media distancia y tener una presencia considerablemente mayor dentro del área. Ya no es el jugador que debe generar el juego por sí solo: esa responsabilidad ahora se distribuye entre los jugadores que forman el rombo. The role change also lets him receive far more often in positional superiority, which in turn gives him the platform for shots from range and considerably more presence inside the box. 04

Un costado que los defensores no pueden resolver A Flank Defenders Can't Solve

El movimiento también abre espacio real por fuera. El lateral rival ya no sabe si seguir a Firmino cuando se mete hacia dentro o mantener su posición y esperar la llegada del lateral propio en superposición. Esa duda crea una segunda consecuencia del movimiento: la posición de Firmino no solo libera al propio jugador, sino que modifica las decisiones defensivas del rival. Posición interior de Firmino Firmino's interior position

Del extremo al interior From The Flank Into The Interior

El valor del movimiento está en la relación entre amplitud y ocupación interior. Firmino parte desde el costado derecho, pero su movimiento hacia dentro crea un rombo central junto a los dos 6 y Braudilio. Al mismo tiempo, mantiene una duda para el lateral rival: seguirlo hacia dentro o proteger el espacio exterior. The value of the movement comes from the relationship between width and interior occupation. Firmino starts from the right side, but his movement inside creates a central diamond with the two 6s and Braudilio. At the same time, it creates a decision for the opposing full-back: follow him inside or protect the outside space.

LECTURA TÁCTICA TACTICAL READ

Una modificación pequeña con efectos en cadena A Small Change With Chain-Reaction Effects

Firmino ya no necesita ser el jugador que recibe constantemente entre líneas y resuelve bajo presión. Al moverse desde el falso extremo derecho hacia posiciones interiores, puede llegar a espacios más favorables, mientras que Braudilio puede ocupar el espacio del #10 y el equipo conserva amplitud por fuera. Firmino no longer needs to be the player constantly receiving between the lines and solving under pressure. By moving from the false right-wing position into interior spaces, he can arrive in more favorable areas while Braudilio occupies the #10 space and the team maintains width outside.
