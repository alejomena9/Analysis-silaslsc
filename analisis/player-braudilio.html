Braudilio Rodríguez

Mobile Forward (CF / #10 / Winger)
In One Line

His movement across three positions isn't just about his own numbers — it's the space that's been fueling Lexington's recent run of wins.

The Value Of Positional Freedom

What has Braudilio actually given this team during the recent run of wins? The main reason comes down to his mobility — his ability to rotate through different positions, playing at different moments as a center-forward, a #10, or a winger.

Space For Everyone Else

That mobility does more than create chances for Braudilio himself — it opens space for teammates to attack. Firmino has directly benefited from the pockets Braudilio's movement creates, most visibly in the games against Tampa Bay, especially on the opening goal. Late-arriving runners from deep have benefited too — Ferri's first goal against San Antonio came from exactly this kind of space.

Why It Matters

This one player's mobility is generating space, rotations and scoring chances — not just for himself, but for everyone moving around him.
