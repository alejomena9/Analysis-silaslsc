The Read Held — And Then Some

The plan was to force errors high up and attack the interior channel between Orange County's double pivot and back four. From the ninth minute, that's where the game went: a three-against-two break that Firmino carried and Goodrum finished at the back post, then Epps turning and shooting from outside the box off Firmino's pass at 15'.

Firmino Between The Lines

Braudílio started on the bench and came on at 55', so the central combinations ran through Firmino. He assisted the first two goals and scored the fourth, assisted by Malcolm Fry. In between, Goodrum headed in a Ferri corner at 24'.

A Second Hat Trick In Eight Days

Goodrum completed his hat trick at 49' from Epps' cross, a week after his three in Oakland. Alex Rando made eight saves, including a penalty at 61', or the score would have been bigger still. Cielo Tschantret's first USL Championship goal, a rebound after Goodrum hit the post, made it six.

Context

Yaniv Bazini, the Orange County striker the pre-match plan was built to contain, didn't play. Orange County managed one shot on target all night.

Conclusion

Everything the pre-match report asked for happened, and early: pressure that forced mistakes, and a channel behind the double pivot that Orange County never closed. With Bazini absent the risk in behind never materialised — one shot on target all night — and Lexington scored more goals than in any game of their Championship era.
