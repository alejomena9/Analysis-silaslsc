45 Seconds

The plan was clear: press the build-up, force turnovers, then attack the pocket behind the double pivot. It took 45 seconds. Goodrum chased a pass along Monterey Bay's touchline, intercepted it and found Firmino for the fastest goal in club history.

Not The Diagonal — A Long Throw

Monterey Bay were level four minutes later, and not through the long diagonal the report had flagged: Zack Farnsworth scored from a long throw into the box. It stayed 1–1 until half-time.

What the plan didn't stop

The danger flagged before the match was the long diagonal behind our full-backs. Both Monterey Bay goals came another way: a long throw into the box at 5' and a late goal at 85' with the game already 3–1.

Won By The Press, Again

The second half was decided by the same idea as the first minute. At 74' Epps picked a Monterey Bay pocket and Molloy rolled a weighted ball across the ground into Goodrum's run, behind the pivot. At 80' Epps won the ball again and finished Braudílio's pass. Diego Otoya's late goal made the scoreline closer than the game.

Conclusion

The press did exactly what the pre-match report asked of it — both decisive goals and the opener began with a Lexington player winning the ball. What the plan didn't cover was the set-piece threat: the long throw was Monterey Bay's best route back into the game. Three straight wins, and third in the West.
