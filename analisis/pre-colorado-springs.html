April 4

Crosses + explosive transitions
In One Line

Colorado Springs score two ways — crowding the box on crosses, or exploding forward the instant they win the ball in zone two.

Two Clear Scoring Patterns

Colorado Springs have two reliable ways of scoring. The first is through crosses: they send a heavy volume of balls into the box and consistently commit numbers into the final third to make sure those crosses have targets.

The second is the fast defense-to-attack transition. They have a center-forward who is genuinely dangerous in 1v1 situations, and the team as a whole is explosive the moment they win the ball back — especially when that recovery happens in zone two, where they turn defense into attack almost instantly.

Prediction

Limit turnovers in zone two and defend the box with real numbers on crosses — take away those two release valves and Colorado Springs lose most of their scoring threat.
