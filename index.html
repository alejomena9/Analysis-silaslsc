02 / Team Tactics
Team Tactics

The Molloy & Ferri Variable

Rotating Diamond
What Changed Dropping the fixed double-6 — Ferri and Aaron no longer sit at the same height at all times, one covers while the other steps forward.
Effect Combined with a more mobile Braudilio up top, Lexington now forms a rotating diamond (dropping 6, Firmino as a 10, a tucking winger, and Braudilio) behind the opposing markers.

Conclusion — The interior superiority this creates also opens the flanks for faster, more direct transitions.

March 29

vs. Monterrey FC

August 1

3–2 (W)
Tariq — width off the bench Epps — isolated 1v1 Goodrum & Molloy — deep runners
Direct build-up
Attack Both goals came exactly where the plan pointed — winning the second ball off an organized press, then attacking Monterrey's weaker left side. Tariq's introduction stretched their backline, isolating Epps in his ideal 1v1 spot; the third goal followed from a corner rebound born of that same pressure.
Defense Monterrey's two goals came from the exact space flagged pre-match — direct balls behind the right-back after a low-risk build-up designed to avoid the high press.

Conclusion — A comeback win — with the game in hand, an extra midfielder for control (rather than staying exposed in transition) would have been the better closing move.

vs. Phoenix Rising FC

August 8

1–0 (W)
Tarik — width off the bench Epps — freed by the switch Burks & Brown — shut down transitions
Positional build-up
Attack Lexington controlled the game for 45 minutes without reward — occupying all five lanes but disconnected in the box, with Goodrum repeatedly left isolated on crosses. The fix came from the bench: Tarik's introduction in the 59th added the width the starting XI lacked, opening space behind Phoenix's holding midfielder that Epps exploited. The goal followed four minutes later.
Defense Burks and Brown were the platform that never wavered — absorbing every Phoenix attack and delaying transitions all match. Phoenix finished the game without a single shot on target.

Conclusion — A deserved win built on control and a clean sheet, but the box-connection problem was solved reactively rather than by design — that fix needs to happen before kickoff, not after 59 minutes.
